Offline lyric translation model

The English→Persian and Persian→English models in this pack are the
Firefox Translations "tiny" students released by Mozilla
(https://github.com/mozilla/translations), distributed under the
Mozilla Public License 2.0 (LICENSE-MPL-2.0.txt).

Source Code Form of the model files:
  English→Persian  models/en-fa/h1-2025_c_TYU1BOTByxOnnkAunO7g/student-finetuned/
  Persian→English  models/fa-en/h1-2025_dyvKzoMQToKWHx7bbP-TtQ/student-finetuned/
  in https://storage.googleapis.com/moz-fx-translations-data--303e-prod-translations-data/

Modifications: the float32 checkpoints (final.model.npz.best-chrf.npz) were
converted to Core ML with per-channel int8 weights. The conversion script is
scripts/lyrics-translation-model/convert_firefox_translations.py in the
Primuse repository. The SentencePiece vocabularies (vocab.*.spm) are
unmodified copies of the released vocab.en.spm files.
